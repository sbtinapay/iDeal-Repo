# Active Outlets

***

Go to **Main Menu > Targets > Active Outlet**

Click **+ADD TARGET**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (45).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will now set the start and end dates for the specific target to take effect. Click **SAVE** to continue.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (41).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be redirected back to the list of target after setting dates.

Select the date periods and click the Eye icon to set the targets.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (45).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can now specify the target number outlets for each existing route by category.

Save the target by clicking SAVE, and it will be reflected on your Dashboard.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (46).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
