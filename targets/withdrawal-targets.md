# Withdrawal Targets

Withdrawal targets manage the specific quantities or amount of products or services that a dealer intends to pruchase from a supplir within a given period. These targets are often established to ensure a consistent supply of googs, maintain inventory levels, and optimize purchasing costs. (Supplier to Dealer Targeet).

***

Go to **Main Menu > Targets > Withdrawal Targets**

From the Withdrawal Target lists Click **+ADD TARGET**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (36).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will now set the start and end dates for the specific target to take effect. Click **SAVE** to continue.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (37).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once saved, you will be taken to the withdrawal target list. Click the **Eye icon** to set your desired target.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (38).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Set the desired target for each product/SKU and click **SAVE** to activate the target. The target will appear on your dashboard.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (39).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
