# Sales to Trade

***

Go to **Main Menu > Targets > Sales to Trade**

From the list Click **+ADD TARGET**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (32).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will now set the start and end dates for the specific target to take effect. Click **SAVE** to continue.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (33).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be redirected back to the list of target after setting dates.

Select the date and click the Eye icon to set the targets.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (34).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can now specify the target volume per product/sku for each existing route.

Save the target by clicking **SAVE**, and it will be reflected on the Dashboard & agent’s DMS App.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (35).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
