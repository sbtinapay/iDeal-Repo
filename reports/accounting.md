# Accounting

Payables and receivables are two critical financial reports that provide insights into a business’s cash flow and overall financial health. They help businesses track the money owed to them by customers (receivables) and the money they owe to suppliers and vendors (payables).

### 1. Payable Report

Go to **Main Menu > Reports > Payables**

The ability to filter your payables report by **month**, **year**, or a **custom** date range gives you greater flexibility in analyzing your data.

You have the option to download your report in PDF format.

### 2. Receivables Report

Go to **Main Menu > Reports > Receivables**

Similar to payables, you can filter your receivables report by **month**, **year**, or a **custom** date range, allowing for more in-depth data analysis.

You have the option to download your report in PDF format.
