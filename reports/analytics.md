# Analytics

###

### 1. OpEx Analytics

OPEX stands for **Operational Expenses**. It refers to the ongoing costs a business incurs to maintain its day-to-day operations.

Go to **Main Menu > Analytics > Operational Expenses**

You can filter the analytics by Today, This Week, This Month, This Year, or Per Year.

The following overview will be displayed based on your filter selection:

* Sales
* Expenses
* Payroll
* Average Volume
* Profit vs OpEx Line Chart
* Productive Routes (Pie Graph)
* Top Outlets

### 2. Profit & Loss

Go to **Main Menu > Reports > Profit & Loss**

Profit & loss reports include line charts that visually depict the relationship between expenses and income over a specific period (monthly, yearly, or custom).

Your report can be downloaded as an image file.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (31).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 3. Sales Analytics

Sales analytics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting sales data to gain insights into a business’s sales performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

Go to **Main Menu > Analytics > Sales**

You can filter the analytics by Today, This Week, This Month, This Year, or Per Year.

The following overview will be displayed based on your filter selection:

* Sales in money value
* Sales in Volume
* Average per day
* Average Volume
* Annualized Sales (Line Chart)
* Productive Routes (Pie Graph)
* Top Outlets
