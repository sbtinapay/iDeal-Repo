# Add Income/Expense Category

### 1. Adding Category

Go to **Main Menu > Basic Accounting > Category**

You can add more income/expense categories from the existing list. Click **+REGISTER NEW CATEGORY**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (16).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will determine the name of your category and what type **(Income or Expense).**

Click **SAVE** to register
