# Product Empties

### 1. Main Menu > Products > Product Empties

From the product empties list, Click **+NEW EMEPTIES** to register empties.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (47).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Action Legends:**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (49).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 2. Register New Empties

Empties can be categorize by sizes or per sku depending on your operational standard. (e.g 1000ml)

Complete the following fields with the requested information, and then select **SAVE**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (48).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

After saving this will be added on the empties list.

### 3. View and Create Empty Transaction

On the Empties List, click the Eye icon <img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (50).png" alt="" data-size="line"> to Add Transaction.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (51).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Within this section, you can view and manage the movement of empties by selecting **+NEW TRANSACTION**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (52).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Fill in the information and click SAVE.
