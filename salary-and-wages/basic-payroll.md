# Basic Payroll

{% hint style="info" %}
_Note: To avoid redundancy in payroll processing, ensure that all payroll categories are clearly identified. Refer to ‘_[_Employee & Payroll Category’_](employee-and-payroll-category.md) _for instructions._
{% endhint %}

### 1. Add Payroll Period

Go to **Main Menu > Salary & Wages > Payroll**

Click **+ADD PAYROLL**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (59).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Determine your payroll period. Click **SAVE** to confirm.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (60).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 2. Add Payroll

Go to **Main Menu > Salary & Wages > Payroll**

Determine the payroll period you want to process

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (61).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Include all employees in your designated payroll period.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (62).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Determine their Earnings and Deductions. Click **SAVE** to process.

Continue repeating these steps until all required employees have been entered.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (63).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 3. Approve Payroll

{% hint style="info" %}
_Note: Payroll approval is subject to your user permissions and role._
{% endhint %}

From the payroll list. You may choose payroll period.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (64).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once you’ve thoroughly checked the payroll, proceed to approval by clicking the APPROVED button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (65).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 4. Release Payroll

Once approved, you can release the payroll.

From the payroll list. You may choose payroll period you want to release.

{% hint style="info" %}
_Note: You can only release payroll if hte status is APPROVED._
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click **RELEASED** to release the payroll.

Status now will change to **RELEASED** in payroll list.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
